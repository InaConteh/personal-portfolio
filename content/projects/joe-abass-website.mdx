## Problem

The client needed a digital presence that stood out from standard template sites, requiring custom animation timelines, fast initial load times, and flawless mobile responsiveness.

## Process

Built on React 19 + Vite for fast iteration and bundle optimization. Integrated GSAP for hardware-accelerated micro-interactions and Lenis for smooth momentum scrolling.

- Component-driven modular architecture for rapid section expansion.
- Hardware-accelerated GSAP timelines with prefers-reduced-motion fallbacks.
- Fluid clamp typography eliminating rigid breakpoints.
- Deployment pipeline on Vercel's edge network.

## Outcome

Achieved a 99/100 Lighthouse Performance score, under 1.2s First Contentful Paint, and 100% positive client feedback.
