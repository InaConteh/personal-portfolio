## Problem

Talented regional athletes lacked structured digital portfolios and verified match statistics accessible to international scouts and agency managers.

## Process

Engineered a lightweight vanilla JS & CSS Grid portal optimized for the low-bandwidth mobile networks common in West Africa, ensuring fast loading over 3G connections.

- Player profile directory with filterable positions and statistics.
- Optimized media gallery with progressive image loading.
- Direct inquiry system connecting agency representatives with talent scouts.
- Accessible, high-contrast dark aesthetic tailored for mobile devices.

## Outcome

Established a unified digital showcase for regional sports talent.
